You can permanently delete your Dicebound account, and the data that goes with it, at any time. Dicebound is published by Nexleaf. There are two ways to do it: in the game, or by email if you no longer have the game installed.

Deletion is permanent. Your progress, items and friends list cannot be restored afterwards, and we cannot undo it for you.

Option 1: Delete in the game

This is the fastest way and works for guest accounts as well as accounts linked to Google Play Games or Apple. You need an internet connection.

  1. Open Dicebound and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and tap Continue.
  4. Tap Delete forever to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are returned to the sign-in screen. If the online part of the deletion cannot be completed (for example, because you are offline), nothing is deleted and the game tells you so. Try again when you are connected.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the game, email us and we will delete the account for you.

  1. Email {{email}} with the subject Dicebound account deletion request.
  2. Include your player name with its number tag (for example Name#1234) and, if you have it, your player ID. Both are shown in the game under Settings > Account.
  3. Tell us how you signed in: Google Play Games, Apple, or as a guest.

Email a deletion request

We may ask a follow-up question to confirm the account is yours, so that nobody can delete another player's account. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your player ID, player name and the link to your Google Play Games or Apple sign-in.
  • Your online save: all progress stored online, including dice, cards, levels, currencies, chests and cosmetics.
  • Your social data: your friends list, incoming and outgoing friend requests, and your block list. Any lobby you are in is left and pending invitations are withdrawn.
  • Data on your device (in-game deletion only): the account's save files and backups, your saved sign-in session and the local co-op log.

If you ask by email, we delete the online data. Game data left on a device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

What may be kept

  • Emails you sent us, including your deletion request and any player reports, which we keep as a record of the request and to keep the game safe, for no longer than 24 months.
  • Provider backups and logs. Our service provider, Unity, may hold residual copies in its backups and server logs for a limited time under its own policies before they are removed.
  • Your Google or Apple account. Deleting your Dicebound account does not delete your Google Play Games or Apple account. Those are managed with Google and Apple.
  • Information needed to meet a legal obligation, if any applies.

Deleting only some data

You can remove friends and unblock players in the game at any time without deleting your account. To ask for other specific data to be deleted without closing your account, email {{email}} and tell us what you would like removed.
