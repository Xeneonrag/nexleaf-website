Dicebound lets you add friends and play co-op with other people. These guidelines explain what we expect from every player. They are part of the Terms of Service.

Be a good teammate

  • Treat other players with respect, whether you win or lose.
  • Do not harass, bully or threaten anyone. That includes spamming emotes, friend requests or invitations at someone who does not want them.
  • Do not deliberately ruin a co-op match for your teammate.

Player names

Your player name is visible to friends and teammates. Names must not:

  • contain slurs, hate speech, sexual content or threats;
  • insult or target a person or group;
  • pretend to be another player, Nexleaf, or a member of staff;
  • advertise anything or contain personal information such as a phone number.

The game blocks many offensive names automatically and hides them from other players. No filter catches everything, so please report names that slip through.

Play fair

  • Do not cheat or use hacks, bots or modified versions of the game.
  • Do not exploit bugs. If you find one, tell us at {{email}}.
  • Do not edit your save data to give yourself currency, items or progress.
  • Do not buy, sell or share accounts.

Staying safe

  • Never share your password or sign-in details. Nexleaf will never ask for them.
  • Only accept friend requests from people you want to play with.
  • Dicebound has no text or voice chat. If someone asks you to move to another app to talk and you are not comfortable, you do not have to.

Block a player

  • During a co-op match: open your teammate's profile and choose Block communication. Their emotes are muted straight away and they can no longer send you friend requests or invitations.
  • From your friends list: choose Block. This removes them as a friend and blocks them.
  • You can see and undo your blocks under Friends > Requests.

Report a player

Use the Report button on a teammate's profile or in your friends list. Pick a reason (offensive name, cheating, or harassment and other problems) and your email app opens a report addressed to us with the details already filled in. Add anything that helps and send it. You can also email {{email}} directly.

Please do not send false reports.

What happens when rules are broken

We read every report and aim to review it within a few days. Depending on what happened and how serious it is, we may warn the player, reset their name, remove unfairly gained progress or items, restrict their online features, or suspend or delete their account.

If you believe action was taken against your account by mistake, email {{email}} and we will look again.
