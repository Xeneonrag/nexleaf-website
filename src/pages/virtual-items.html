This page explains how virtual currencies and items work in Dicebound, a game by Nexleaf. It is part of the Terms of Service.

Dicebound does not currently sell anything for real money. Prices you may see in the shop are placeholders for items planned for a later update, and they cannot be bought. Everything in the game today is earned by playing.

Virtual currencies and items

The game includes virtual currencies (such as Gold, Gems, Ore, Energy and event tokens) and virtual items (such as dice, cards, chests and cosmetics). Together these are "virtual items".

  • Virtual items can be used only inside Dicebound.
  • They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money, goods or services.
  • They cannot be sold, traded or transferred to another player or account.
  • You have a licence to use them in the game; you do not own them.

How you get them

Virtual items are earned through play: winning matches, completing quests, levelling up, opening chests, taking part in events, and one-time rewards such as the gift for linking your account.

Random rewards

Some rewards are random. Chests give a random selection of items, and monthly event minigames give random prizes. These are opened or played with currencies earned in the game. Where the game shows the possible contents or chances for a reward, you can see them before you spend. REVIEW: full odds are not yet shown for every random reward. If real-money purchases are added, both stores require the odds to be disclosed before purchase.

Changes to virtual items

Dicebound is still being developed. We may change how virtual items work, rebalance them, change how they are earned, or remove them. We may also reset progress during testing periods before the full release; when we do, we will say so in the game or on this website.

If you lose your items

  • Guest accounts are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the game, clear its data or change device, guest progress and items are lost and we cannot restore them. Link your account to back up your progress.
  • Deleting your account permanently removes all of its virtual items. This cannot be undone.
  • If items go missing because of a bug, email {{email}} with your player name and tag and what happened, and we will do what we can.

Purchases and refunds

Because nothing is currently sold for real money, there is nothing to refund at present.

If real-money purchases are added in a future update, they will be made through the app store on your device (Google Play or the Apple App Store). Payments and refunds would be handled by that store under its own refund policy, and this page will be updated before any purchase goes on sale.
