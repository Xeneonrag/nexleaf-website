This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Dicebound (the "game"), how it is used, who processes it, and the choices you have. Dicebound is developed and published by Nexleaf ("Nexleaf", "we", "us"), an independent game developer based in North Carolina, United States.

The short version

  • Dicebound has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no free-text or voice chat.
  • We collect only what the game needs to run accounts, save your progress, and let you play with friends.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the game or by email.

Information we collect

What is collected depends on how you play.

Account and sign-in

  • Guest play. When you choose "Play as Guest", the game creates an anonymous player account with our online service provider, Unity. It has a random player ID and is not linked to your name, email address or any other account.
  • Google Play Games (Android) or Sign in with Apple (iOS). If you sign in or link your account with one of these, Unity receives an identifier for your Google Play Games or Apple account so it can recognise you when you return. We do not receive your Google or Apple password. On Android, the game also reads your Google Play Games display name; it is stored on your device to label your sign-in and may be used as your starting player name until you choose one. On iOS, the game does not ask Apple for your name or email address.

Player name and profile

You choose a player name, which is shown with a number tag (for example Name#1234). Your profile also includes the picture, banner and other cosmetics you select from the game's built-in art. You cannot upload your own images.

Game progress

Your progress is saved on your device: level, experience, trophies, currencies (such as Gold and Gems), dice, cards, decks, quests, event progress, cosmetics and settings. If you are signed in with Google Play Games or Apple, a copy is also saved online with Unity Cloud Save so you can restore it. Guest progress is stored only on your device and is not backed up.

Friends and co-op play

  • Your friends list, friend requests you send or receive, and players you block.
  • Your presence while you are online: whether you are online, what you are doing (for example in the menu, in a lobby or in a match), your game version, your current difficulty and wave, any profile badges, and whether you have an open lobby.
  • Co-op invitations and replies. These are fixed message types, not written messages.
  • Temporary co-op lobbies, which hold your player name and game version while the lobby exists.

Network information

Like any online service, the game's online features process your IP address in order to connect you. This includes Unity's services and the relay service that connects co-op players. The game itself does not store your IP address.

Support and player reports

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. When you report a player in the game, your email app opens a message addressed to us that is pre-filled with the reported player's name and ID, the reason, your own player name and ID, the game version and the date. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Data stored only on your device

The game keeps your save files, your sign-in session and a small technical log of co-op connections on your device. These are not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

Dicebound does not collect or request:

  • your real name, email address (unless you email us), postal address or phone number;
  • your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone;
  • an advertising ID or a device identifier;
  • payment or card details. The game does not currently offer real-money purchases.

The game contains no advertising software and no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or cross-app tracking software.

How we use information

  • To create and sign you in to your account.
  • To save, back up and restore your progress.
  • To provide the friends system and co-op play, including showing your name, profile and presence to your friends and teammates.
  • To keep the game safe and fair, including acting on player reports and filtering offensive names.
  • To answer support, privacy and deletion requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles.

What other players can see

  • Friends can see your player name, online status, current activity, difficulty and wave, profile badges, and whether you have an open lobby.
  • Co-op teammates can see your player card (name, picture, banner, badges, level, runs played and highest wave) and the deck you bring into the match.
  • Anyone who knows your exact player name and tag, or your player ID, can send you a friend request. You can decline it or block them.

There are no public leaderboards and no public player search.

Storage and security

Online account data is stored on Unity's servers, which may be located in the United States and other countries. Connections between the game and Unity's services are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and co-op connections through Unity Relay use encrypted DTLS. Game progress is saved by the game on your own device, so anyone with access to your device may be able to reach it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long information is kept

  • Account, online save, friends and blocks: kept until you delete your account.
  • Presence, invitations and lobbies: temporary. Invitations expire after about 90 seconds, and lobbies exist only while they are in use.
  • Data on your device: kept until you delete your account in the game or remove the game and its data.
  • Support emails and player reports: kept for as long as needed to handle the request and keep the game safe, and for no longer than 24 months.

After deletion, our service providers may keep residual copies in their backups and logs for a limited time under their own policies.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account inside the game at Settings > Account > Delete Account, or ask us to do it by email. Deletion removes your account, player name, online save, friends list, friend requests and blocks, and cannot be undone. Full details are on the Account Deletion page.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play as a guest without linking a Google or Apple account.
  • You can block and report other players, and remove friends, at any time.
  • You can delete your account at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict how it is used. To make a request, email {{email}}. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account holder before acting on it, and we will not treat you differently for making a request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on the need to provide the game you asked to play (accounts, saves, friends and co-op), our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and working, and compliance with legal obligations.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email {{email}} and we will delete it.
