Account and progress

How do I keep my progress safe?

Guest progress is stored only on your device and is lost if you uninstall the game or clear its data. To back it up, go to Settings > Account and link your account with Google Play Games (Android) or Apple (iOS). Linked accounts are saved online and can be restored by signing in again.

I lost my guest progress. Can you restore it?

Unfortunately not. Guest progress is never sent to us, so there is no copy to restore.

Playing with friends

How do I add a friend?

Open the Friends panel and send a request using your friend's player name and number tag (Name#1234) or their player ID.

We can't connect in co-op

Both players need an internet connection and the same version of the game. Update the game on both devices, then try a new invitation. Invitations expire after about 90 seconds.

Report or block a player

Use the Report and Block buttons on a teammate's profile or in your friends list. Reports open an email to us with the details filled in. You can also email us directly.

Delete your account

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account in the game, or email us with the subject "Dicebound account deletion request".
